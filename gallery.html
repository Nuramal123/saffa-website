About My SUKSIS Experience

Joining SUKSIS has given me valuable experience in leadership, discipline, and teamwork. Through marching training and group activities, I learned how to communicate effectively, follow instructions, and work together as a team.

This experience also helped me build confidence, improve physical discipline, and strengthen friendship with my fellow members. It is one of the most meaningful parts of my university life.
